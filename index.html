A sound is made of up to 4 layers that play together. Every layer has the same sections: Source (raw material) → Pitch → Volume (when and how long) → Brightness (filter) → Movement → Character. Switch layers off or tap ▶ on a layer to hear what each one adds.

About Listen

Listen creates cinematic sound effects right in your browser — from 8-bit blips to explosions, generator shutdowns, sweeping whooshes and goosebump choirs. Record your own sounds and transform them with one tap.

Everything is synthesized and processed on your device. No audio is uploaded, there is no tracking and no cookies; projects are stored only in this browser's storage.
